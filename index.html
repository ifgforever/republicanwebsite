City of Chicago

    Missed the deadline? From Oct 7, Illinois lets you register and vote the same day at an early voting site, or at your polling place on Election Day. Bring two IDs, one showing your current address. Not in Chicago? Suburban Cook voters should check dates with the Cook County Clerk; everyone else, with your county clerk.

    Cook County

    Chicago & suburban Cook

    Republicans are running for three seats on the Water Reclamation District board. In three other countywide races no Republican is on the ballot. There you can mark a non-Democrat instead, or skip the race.

    No Republican running

    In these races the Libertarian is the only name besides the Democrat. Tap one to add it to your ballot, or leave the race blank.

    Only a Democrat is running

    • ClerkMonica Gordon (D, incumbent)
    • TreasurerMaria Pappas (D, incumbent)
    • Board of Review2nd District: Elizabeth "Liz" Nicholson (D). Other districts: check your sample ballot.
    • Water Reclamation District, 2-year termCameron "Cam" Davis (D)

    No Republican is on the ballot for Board President. Eric Wallace ran as a write-in in the March primary, but he does not appear on the Cook County Clerk's write-in list for November. Check your sample ballot.

    County Board: all 17 commissioner seats are up. Republicans are running in several districts. Your sample ballot shows your district and who is running in it.

    You can skip a race. Leaving one blank does not affect the rest of your ballot. Every vote you cast for governor, Senate and Congress still counts in full.

    Springfield

    State House & Senate

    Every state representative is on this ballot, and in many districts your state senator is too. Democrats hold supermajorities in both chambers, and these are the races that change that. Your sample ballot lists your districts and who is running.

    Judges

    Retention · Yes or No

    Near the end of your ballot is a long list of sitting judges asking to keep their seats. Each one needs 60% Yes to stay. These are easy to skip in the booth, so look the names up on your sample ballot before you go and decide ahead of time.
